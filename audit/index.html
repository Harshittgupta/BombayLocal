Unlink Audit

Unlink makes claims about who may issue passes, which pass list is current, and which verification key scanners use. This page checks each claim live, straight from Ethereum and Arkiv, in your browser. Nothing here comes from the Unlink team's servers.

    Issuers

    Every address the Registry authority ever added. A suspended issuer can never be re-added. chain values come from the Registry contract, arkiv values from entities whose creator and owner are that issuer.
